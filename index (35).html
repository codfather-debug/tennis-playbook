The Mental Game

Technical skill gets you on court. Mental skill wins matches.

4 Mental Pillars

The same metrics tracked in your match reflections

Between-Point Routine

Do this after every single point — win or lose

1

React

Allow yourself one brief reaction — positive or neutral. Never dwell.

2

Release

Turn away from the net. Exhale. Look at your strings or walk calmly.

3

Reset

Bounce the ball, pick your serve target, commit to a pattern.

4

Ready

Split step before the serve. Eyes locked. Trust the plan.

Momentum Management

You're on a roll

Shorten between-point time. Serve quickly. Don't let your opponent reset.

Opponent is on a roll

Slow everything down. Bounce the ball more. Take a full breath. Break their rhythm.

Big point (break point, set point)

Go to your highest-percentage shot. This is not the time to try something new.

After a double fault

Reset completely before the next point. One serve at a time. Pick a target, not a result.

Down a set

Wipe the slate. The second set is a new match. Go back to what's working.

Self-Talk Protocol

Replace negative self-talk in real time

Don't miss this

→ Hit to the backhand corner

I'm going to double fault

→ Spin it in to the body

I always choke on this point

→ Play the ball, not the score

I can't beat this guy

→ Focus on the next ball

I'm so bad at this

→ I'm going to hit 3 in a row

Body Language

Your body broadcasts your mental state — to yourself and your opponent

Walk tall

Head up, shoulders back between every point — regardless of the score.

Pump your fist

After big points won. Controlled celebration builds momentum.

Slow your walk

When you want to slow the game down, walk slowly and deliberately.

Never drop your head

Slumped shoulders broadcast weakness to your opponent.

Stay still at baseline

Stillness before the serve signals control and focus.

Pre-Match Prep

🎯

Arrive with a game plan. Know your first-strike patterns before you start.

🧘

Warm up at match pace — not 50%. Your body needs to be ready from point one.

📋

Set a process goal (e.g. 'first strike on every 2nd serve') not just 'win'.

🚶

Use the walk-on time to breathe and get focused. Not to worry about the opponent.

💤

Sleep and nutrition matter more than last-minute drilling. Arrive rested.

Common Mental Mistakes

⚠ Changing a winning game plan

If it's working, keep doing it. The temptation to be clever costs more matches than anything.

⚠ Going for too much on big points

Big points call for your best shot, not your riskiest. Consistency wins under pressure.

⚠ Thinking about the score instead of the ball

The score is information. The ball is the job. Focus on the ball.

⚠ Reacting visibly to bad calls or errors

Your reaction is your opponent's best weapon. Give them nothing.

⚠ Playing not to lose instead of playing to win

Defensive tennis feels safe but produces errors. Commit to every shot.
