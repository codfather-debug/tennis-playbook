Court Zones

Kill Zone

Around the service line

Finish the point. All attacking-zone rules apply. Look to volley or put away.

Attack Zone

Inside the baseline

Court has shortened — keep the ball within 3 ft over the net. Don't let it sail long.

Neutral Zone

Behind baseline, comfortable

Focus on depth. Hit 3–6 ft over the net with topspin to push opponent back.

Defend Zone

6+ feet behind the baseline

Hit at least 6 ft over the net. Prioritize consistency, depth, and recovery time.

Patterns of Play

🎯

The 2-1

Shot 1

Deep to C+ — push opponent behind the baseline.

Shot 2

Wide to D — pull them off the court, opening the far side.

Shot 3

To A — the unspectacular winner into the open court.

This is your default point-building pattern.

🛡️

The Backhand Cage

Step 1

Use your forehand (sword) to attack their backhand (shield).

Step 2

Make them hit 4 backhands in a row — no relief.

Step 3

Wait for the short ball and put it away.

Counter: if opponent uses this on you, hit backhand down the line without giving up position.

⚡

Serve + 1 Forehand

Serve

Out wide or into the body to create an angle.

+ 1

Run around the reply and hit a forehand — forehands win 2× more than backhands.

Tip

Attack the Deuce or Ad side twice in a row — removes opponent's anticipation.

70% of all points end in 1–4 shots. Win here, win the match.
